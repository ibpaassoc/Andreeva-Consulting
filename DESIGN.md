# Andreeva Consulting design direction

This is a bilingual, editorial brand site for beauty professionals navigating U.S. licensing. Its primary job is to earn trust and make a free consultation easy to book.

The supplied visual reference establishes the identity: white/ivory editorial surfaces, deep navy serif headlines, restrained antique gold actions, fine rules, and the founder portrait. The broad, photo-led hero is the signature element; subsequent sections stay quieter. Avoid invented badges, fake testimonials, aggressive gradients, and unsupported claims.

Runtime tokens live in `app/globals.css`: paper `#fffefd`, ivory `#f7f4ef`, navy `#14263a`, ink `#283443`, gold `#765b37`, rule `#ddd7ce`. Cormorant Garamond serves display text and Manrope body and utility text. Section headings and eyebrows share one scale; buttons share one component style. Responsive structure preserves the same content and booking action.

Motion follows an editorial rise-and-fall rhythm: the hero opens with a controlled image-and-type reveal, Services becomes the signature desktop scene through a native-scroll, pinned horizontal folio, Process uses a quieter pinned progression, and the consultation and footer provide the closing conversion and brand moments. `lib/motion.ts` owns shared media queries, easing, timing, ScrollTrigger registration, section-label treatment, and reveal helpers. Components scope GSAP through `useGSAP`, use `gsap.matchMedia()` for responsive and reduced-motion behavior, and keep scroll state out of React. Prefer transform, opacity, and clip-path; mobile replaces pinning and dense choreography with normal-flow horizontal and rollout reveals. Service cards remain fully readable and non-overlapping: desktop presents them sequentially on a horizontal track, while tablet, mobile, JavaScript-failure, and reduced-motion states retain the established grid. Supporting sections vary direction and reveal geometry without competing with the two pinned scenes. Reduced-motion mode keeps content immediately available and disables decorative parallax, pinning, counting, magnetic movement, and step transitions. Small controls use CSS transitions.

The hero image is one full-bleed photographic surface, softened by a continuous left-to-right veil behind the copy; never introduce a separate colored rectangle or a hard image boundary. At narrow widths, place the image above the copy and let the three facts form legible rows. Service summaries stay equal-height within each grid row; their longer details open in a focused, scrollable sheet without reflowing neighboring cards. On mobile, the compact header retains the logo and language choice while a full-height editorial menu exposes icon-led, descriptive destinations and separates the booking action from navigation. Its panel and rows enter as one restrained GSAP sequence, close in reverse, lock background scrolling, and become immediate under reduced-motion preference. These are layout behaviors, not new color or type tokens; `app/globals.css` remains the canonical runtime token source.

The client reviews section uses a compact editorial introduction and a prominent layered testimonial card. Original review imagery is paired with readable, locale-specific quote text. Desktop navigation sits directly below the card; on narrow screens, the card stacks its image above the quote and changes through horizontal swipes. Keyboard arrow keys and visible arrow buttons remain available when the carousel is focused. Drag rotation stays under three degrees, and the rear sheet advances from a subtle `0.94` scale. A restrained directional crossfade links cards, while reduced-motion preference removes the tween.

The consultation flow is a deliberate two-path decision surface rather than one generic booking form. Each option uses a quiet, document-inspired still life, names its duration and price before the action, and keeps the relevant legal boundary beside the decision. General booking actions open a right-side consultation picker, while the named consultation cards skip directly to that consultation's Cal.com calendar in the same drawer. Opening the native dialog gently recedes the page and reveals the panel with a clipped edge; the two internal steps cross horizontally around an animated progress line. On mobile, both consultation and service detail drawers become bottom sheets that dismiss by downward swipe, backdrop press, or Escape; horizontal service swipes retain visible arrow-button alternatives. Narrow layouts use alternating card offsets, restrained edge circles, and shallow stacked surfaces to distinguish sections without changing the editorial palette. GSAP choreographs the drawer and step transitions, with equivalent immediate states under reduced motion.
